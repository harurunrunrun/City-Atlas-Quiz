HOW IT WORKS

About this quiz

Find the named city among four pins. In Streak, each round picks a country with equal probability. For a focused session, choose Country practice or Weak cities. Letters run from northeast toward southwest, using the cities’ actual map positions. After every answer, all four names are revealed.

The city pool

City pools before and after expansion

Maps & coverage

Boundaries, colors, place names, and country/region groupings are used for geographic learning and navigation. They do not express a position on sovereignty, territorial claims, or disputed or undetermined status. Maps use generalized Natural Earth boundaries with selected disputed areas shown without affiliation. Country/region choices organize the quiz and do not determine sovereignty. Dispute coverage is not exhaustive.

Three ways to play

Streak picks a new random country each round and ends after a wrong answer. Country practice shuffles every city in your chosen country and asks each exactly once, even if you get it wrong. Weak cities makes one shuffled pass through cities whose latest answer was wrong, using the weak list at the start of the pass. A correct answer in any mode removes that city from the weak list. Choices always come from the full country pool.

Local names only

The switch applies to all quiz modes and the Study atlas. It hides English city translations and romanizations while keeping local names that are themselves English. Source-spelling annotations remain. Ambiguous local names use neutral region and place codes when a sourced local region name is unavailable. Switching changes only the display; it does not restart a question or a pass.

Study atlas

Choose a country/region on the world map to see all its indexed cities. Use the state/province/region selector to focus on a subdivision, or keep All country. Every city has a dot and a searchable list entry. Wheel or trackpad over either atlas map to zoom around the cursor. Country-map zoom limits adapt to city spacing. Labels show local and English names, without a fixed label-count limit; at maximum zoom all visible names are shown. Hover and the shared-coordinate chooser make overlapping places inspectable. Selecting a city highlights it without changing zoom; an off-screen city is panned into view. Studying and returning to the quiz do not record answers or change your progress.

Your score

Your name-display preference, best streak, city answer history, and country passes stay in this browser when local storage is available. They do not sync between devices. There is no online leaderboard, game account, timer, or location tracking.
